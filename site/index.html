Path tracing in the browser

A physically based WebGPU path tracer with a WebGL 2 fallback, OpenPBR materials, TinyBVH acceleration and Intel Open Image Denoise.

      Features

      • WebGPU first, WebGL 2 as fallback

        Unidirectional path tracing with next-event estimation and MIS over rect, sphere, IES spot, directional and HDR environment lights. Without WebGPU it falls back to WebGL 2.

      • OpenPBR 1.1 surface

        F82 metals, anisotropic GGX specular and coat, fuzz, thin-film interference, dispersive transmission with volume scattering, subsurface scattering and ray-marched displacement.

      • Interactive with millions of triangles

        Two-level BVH with binned-SAH BLASes built in parallel Workers; moving instances only refits the TLAS. The 4.6 M-triangle Bugatti: 0.5 s build, 5.3 full-HD spp/s on an M4 Max.

      • Low-noise sampling and denoising

        Owen-scrambled Sobol or blue-noise ZSobol sequences. Albedo- and normal-guided denoising with Intel OIDN (WebGPU) or joint non-local means, progressively or on convergence.

      • Incremental Three.js sync

        THREESync converts standard and physical materials, lights, instancing, skinning and morph targets, and picks up transform, material and light edits on every update, without reconverting the scene.

      • Offline-quality output

        Deterministic converge() with variance-driven adaptive sampling (WebGPU), AgX and ACES tone mapping, bloom, thin-lens depth of field and shadow catchers.
